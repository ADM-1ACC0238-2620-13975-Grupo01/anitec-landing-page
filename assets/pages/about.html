The Product

What AniTec Does

A mobile app for livestock management.

AniTec helps ranchers register their farms, corrals and animals, and helps veterinarians follow the animals of their clients. Both share the information in one app that keeps working with limited signal.

Animals organized by farm and corral, with photo and a complete record
Health records with follow-up dates
Information shared between ranchers and veterinarians
Charts of your herd and of your records
