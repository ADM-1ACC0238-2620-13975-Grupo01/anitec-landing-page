Why AniTec for Ranchers?

What the app helps you do every day.

Individual Records

Identify each animal with a code and record its species, breed, sex, weight, status, farm, corral and a photo.

Follow-up Dates

Save the date of the next check or vaccine so you can see what is pending.

Health Control

Keep the health history of your animals: incidents, vaccines, treatments, diagnoses and prescriptions.

Scan the Ear Tag

Find an animal by scanning its tag with the camera, or by typing its code.

Finances

Record income and expenses and see your balance in soles.

Herd Overview

See how many animals are healthy, under observation or in treatment, and your records by type.
