Key Features

What you can do with AniTec

Everything you need to run your livestock from your phone.

Farms and Animals

Organize your animals by farm and corral. Register them one by one or in batches of up to 500, add a photo and open a complete record.

Health Records

Record incidents, vaccines, treatments and diagnoses, with a follow-up date so nothing is forgotten.

Scan the ear tag

Identify an animal by scanning its code with the camera, or type the code if you prefer.

Works with limited signal

Animals, health records and activities you create without a connection are saved on your phone and sent when it reconnects.

You decide who sees your data

A veterinarian asks for access and you accept, reject or revoke it at any time. Only accepted veterinarians see your animals.

Reminders for every activity

Get a notification at 8:00 on the day an activity is due. Mark it attended or reschedule it from its card.

Sync without duplicates

Changes made offline are sent only once, even if the connection drops halfway. If a record changed elsewhere, you choose which version to keep.

Finances

Record income and expenses in soles and see your balance at any time.

Analytics and Sensors

See charts of your herd's health and records, and the latest readings from your IoT devices.

Readable for everyone

High-contrast colors, buttons that screen readers can name and text that scales up to 200%.
